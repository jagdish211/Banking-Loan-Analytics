# 🏦 Banking & Loan Analytics

An end-to-end **Data Analytics project** analyzing **100,000 loan applications** to understand loan demand, approval performance, customer segments, and repayment risk.

## 🎯 Business Problem

The bank wants to use loan data to improve:

* Loan approval decisions
* Customer targeting
* Loan demand analysis
* Repayment risk monitoring

## 📊 Project Scope

* **100K** loan records
* **14** original columns
* **6** engineered columns
* **30** SQL queries
* **9** Python EDA charts
* **4** Power BI pages
* **16** KPIs
* **7** key findings

## 🛠️ Tools

**Python | Pandas | NumPy | Matplotlib | Seaborn | MySQL | SQL | Power BI | DAX | Excel**

## 📈 Power BI Dashboard

**1. Executive Overview** — Overall loan performance and demand

**2. Loan Approval Analysis** — Approval trends by loan and customer segments

**3. Risk & Repayment Analysis** — Defaults, overdue loans and payment delays

**4. Customer & Loan Analysis** — Customer demographics and loan patterns

## 💡 Key Findings

* Personal Loans have the highest demand with **30,075 applications**.
* Home Loans contribute approximately **59.08% of total loan value**.
* Good credit-score customers have the highest approval rate at **66.94%**.
* Salaried customers have the highest approval rate at **64.24%**.
* Auto Loans have the highest average payment delay at **10.31 days**.
* **4,211** customers have payment delays above 60 days.

## 🚀 Recommendations

* Use **risk-based approval** using multiple customer and loan factors.
* Strengthen **Auto Loan repayment monitoring**.
* Create **early-warning alerts** for high payment delays.
* Monitor **high-risk customer segments**.
* Closely monitor **Home Loan portfolio exposure**.
